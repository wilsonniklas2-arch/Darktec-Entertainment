# DARKTEC — Business Development & Opportunity Map
_Last researched: 11 November 2026_

## Executive direction

DARKTEC should build revenue in two lanes. Start with paid services and partner work in the real event industry; develop the DARKTEC Universe as a longer-term original-IP business. Do not present the Universe as an established commercial franchise yet. Present it as an original creative property in development, supported by a visual archive and clear pitch materials.

Positioning:
- **DARKTEC Entertainment:** live entertainment, event production support, technical show delivery, lighting/media/visual concepts, artist and creative partner coordination.
- **DARKTEC Universe:** original worlds, characters, stories, music and interactive concepts.

## 1. Near-term revenue: technical crew and production work

### A. PRG Crew Portal — strongest immediate route
- Official portal: https://crew.prg.com/de/
- PRG explicitly accepts registration by individual professionals and by companies/personnel providers.
- Listed skill profiles include Lighting Technician, Lighting Designer, Lighting Desk Operator, Video Technician – Media Servers, Media Server Operator, Video/LED, Projection and Media/Content Design.
- Why it fits: directly aligned with event-technology experience in lighting, LED, stage operations and media-server workflows.
- Action: register as an individual professional if applying personally; only use company registration if DARKTEC is actually operating as a suitable registered service provider. Select only skills that can be evidenced.
- Status: target channel identified; no application submitted.

### B. Event production agencies and technical suppliers
Potential subcontracting / project-partner targets (not confirmed vacancies):
- **Eventtation GmbH & Co. KG** — https://www.eventtation.de/ — technical event planning and delivery, complex productions, partner/staff network. Public contact: mail@eventtation.de.
- **MCI Deutschland** — https://www.mci-live.de/ — live experience and brand-event concepts with operational delivery. Contact: germany@wearemci.com.
- **WEMME Events** — https://wemme-events.de/ — lighting, sound, video, stage and nationwide event technical delivery.
- **M+M Event Team** — https://www.m-und-m.com/ — technical, creative and production services including lighting, video, media technology, show and tour support.

Approach: introduce a clearly defined technical profile and availability, not a vague offer to “do everything”. Lead with lighting / media-server / LED-video / stage-tech support, then add show programming or visual concept work where relevant.

## 4. Longer-term revenue: DARKTEC Universe / original IP

### A. Game development and publishing pitches
- **Shueisha Games — Pitch Your Game:** https://shueisha-games.com/en/pitch/
  - Accepts a project proposal and portfolio; work-in-progress builds and video are optional.
  - Proposal should cover game concept, team, development costs, schedule and production members.
- **Marvelous Games — Submit your game:** https://marvelousgames.com/web/de/submit-game
  - Requests game details and pitch materials; asks for a video link and build where available.
- **KADOKAWA Global Licensing:** https://globalrights.kadokawa.co.jp/licensing-guide
  - Licensing applications are for registered companies/business partners, not private individuals.
  - A formal proposal should specify IP, project, territory, schedule, pricing/volume, distribution and company background.

Practical sequence:
1. Build one strong Aethelgard game concept and a concise non-confidential pitch deck.
2. Create a small playable prototype or polished gameplay mock-up before approaching publishers where possible.
3. Keep ownership records for original art, writing, music and character designs.
4. Pitch only DARKTEC-owned original characters and materials. Do not imply ownership of third-party characters, brands, music or artwork.

### B. Other possible Universe revenue models
- Commissioned worldbuilding / character and visual concept packages for clients.
- Original show concepts and audiovisual story experiences for events, venues and brands.
- Licensing original DARKTEC characters, settings and artwork once ownership, documentation and commercial readiness are established.
- Music and sound-design commissions using original or properly licensed material.

These are business models to validate, not confirmed customer demand or guaranteed income.

## 5. Referral / brokerage model — rules before offering it

DARKTEC can potentially introduce a client to a suitable artist, technician, supplier or production partner. Before taking a referral fee or commission:
- Get written agreement from the supplier/partner defining the fee, trigger, payment date, cancellations and repeat business.
- Make the role transparent to the client and partner; do not imply DARKTEC is the employer, technical contractor or production lead unless that is contractually true.
- Define who contracts with the end client, who invoices, who carries insurance and who is responsible for safety, permits and delivery.
- Do not promise availability, prices or technical capacity until the actual supplier confirms them.
- Have German tax/legal setup checked before regular commercial activity or commission invoicing.

## 6. First 30-day action plan

1. **Portfolio:** create a short capability deck with 3 offers: (a) lighting / stage / media-server technician, (b) show & visual concept development, (c) creative partner / artist coordination.
2. **Proof:** collect only shareable work samples, role descriptions, software/equipment skills, certifications and references. Obtain permission before publishing client or ship material.
3. **Outreach:** register with PRG as an individual professional if appropriate; send tailored introductions to 3–5 production partners.
4. **Referral pilot:** secure one written partner agreement before brokering a paid lead.
5. **Universe pitch:** select one original property (Aethelgard) and prepare a concise pitch package; keep it separate from immediate technical-service sales.

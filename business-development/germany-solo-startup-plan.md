# DARKTEC — Germany Solo Founder Startup & Business Plan
**Status:** Pre-startup planning document — no business registration has been filed  
**Prepared:** 12 November 2026  
**Founder model:** One founder / sole proprietor (Einzelunternehmen)  
**Planned country:** Germany  
**Brand:** DARKTEC Entertainment

> This is a practical planning document, not individual legal or tax advice. The exact municipality, tax residence, activity description and contracts must be confirmed before registration or paid operations.

## 1. Executive decision

Start lean as a **sole proprietorship (Einzelunternehmen)** under the founder's own legal name, using **DARKTEC** as the business/brand designation. Do not establish a UG or GmbH at the outset unless a tax adviser or lawyer identifies a clear reason. This avoids notary, commercial-register and annual corporate-compliance costs while validating demand.

Important:
- “Kleingewerbe” is not a separate legal form.
- The founder is personally and generally unlimitedly liable as a sole proprietor.
- A business brand does not replace the legal name on invoices, contracts and the legal notice.
- If the business later grows, takes on staff, assumes production liabilities or brings in co-owners, review conversion to a UG (haftungsbeschränkt) or GmbH.

IHK overview of the sole proprietorship: https://www.ihk.de/berlin/service-und-beratung/recht-und-steuern/firma-und-rechtsformen/rechtsformen-fuer-unternehmen/einzelunternehmen

## 2. Business positioning

### DARKTEC Entertainment
A lean creative and event-production services business with three service pillars:

1. **Production & technical services**
   - Lighting / stage / show-technology support
   - LED, video and media-server operation or programming where qualifications and project scope fit
   - Technical show preparation, documentation and production coordination
   - The founder's own technical services only; do not advertise a staffing pool before one exists

2. **Creative & show development**
   - Show concepts, running orders and creative production support
   - Visual concepts, content planning and audiovisual experience design
   - DJ / music / artist coordination where rights and contracts are clear

3. **DARKTEC Universe — original IP**
   - Original characters, worlds, stories, visual concepts, games and music
   - Initially presented as an original IP in development, not as a proven/licensed franchise
   - Future income through commissions, production services, licensing and publisher/partner deals

### Recommended launch offers
Keep the offer menu narrow and deliverable:

- **Offer A — Technical Show Support:** day/project rate for the founder's own agreed technical services.
- **Offer B — Show & Visual Concept:** fixed-fee concept package with scope, deliverables, revision limit and timeline.
- **Offer C — Creative Production Coordination:** clearly scoped coordination fee for a defined project.
- **Offer D — Partner Introduction:** only after written agreements; transparent referral fee or commission.

Do not promise equipment, crew numbers, availability or delivery capacity that DARKTEC does not own or control.

## 3. Legal form and trading name

Recommended initial form: sole proprietorship (Einzelunternehmen), owner-operated.

For external use, a safe initial presentation is:
**[Founder’s full legal name] — DARKTEC Entertainment**

Avoid describing the business as “GmbH”, “UG”, “Ltd.” or another legal entity unless it has actually been formed. Do not imply a limited-liability structure.

Sole proprietors generally bear personal liability. Obtain professional advice on whether and when a limited-liability entity is appropriate.

## 4. Registered business location — critical precondition

The business municipality must be real and support the actual business establishment. Do not use a convenient postal address or a nominal “paper seat” without a genuine basis.

Before filing, confirm:
- Where the founder actually resides / has a usable business base in Germany
- Where the business is genuinely directed and managed
- Whether the founder's shipboard employment and time abroad affect tax residence, management location or insurance
- Whether the home rental agreement permits business use (usually office/admin use differs from customer traffic, storage or noise-producing activity)
- Which municipality's Gewerbeamt and Finanzamt are competent

The ELSTER guidance says the tax office's local competence is based on the place of management; the place of management is where the entrepreneurial will is formed. https://www.elster.de/eportal/formulare-leistungen/alleformulare/fseeun and https://www.elster.de/eportal/helpGlobal?themaGlobal=help_fsekapg_202401

A prior conversation mentioned Bremerhaven as a possible residence, but that alone does not establish the current legal business location. Do not file until the founder confirms the actual municipality and situation.

## 5. Registration and tax checklist

### Step 1 — Confirm activity and Gewerbe description
Most planned event-production, coordination and commercial creative services are likely to be treated as commercial activity, but the exact scope must be classified. Do not automatically label all creative work as freelance (Freiberufler); the tax office decides classification based on the actual activity.

Draft activity wording for discussion with the local Gewerbeamt / tax adviser:
“Dienstleistungen im Bereich Veranstaltungstechnik und technische Produktionsunterstützung; Konzeption und Koordination von Veranstaltungs- und Showproduktionen; audiovisuelle Konzept- und Kreativleistungen; Vermittlung und Koordination von selbstständigen Dienstleistern und Kooperationspartnern, soweit keine erlaubnispflichtige Tätigkeit ausgeübt wird.”

The final wording must reflect what the founder actually does. Avoid wording that implies employee leasing, security services, financial/insurance brokerage or other regulated activities unless separately authorised.

### Step 2 — Gewerbeanmeldung
- File with the municipality responsible for the genuine place of business.
- Under §14 GewO, a standing trade must be notified to the competent authority when the independent business begins.
- The local authority sets its own process and fee.
- Keep the Gewerbeanmeldung / Gewerbeschein and any correspondence.

Law: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/gewo/__14.html  
General IHK explanation: https://www.ihk.de/karlsruhe/fachthemen/recht/gewerberecht/gewerbeanmeldung-4632354

### Step 3 — Tax registration via ELSTER
- Submit the electronic “Fragebogen zur steuerlichen Erfassung für Einzelunternehmen”.
- The start-up portal says this must be sent within one month of opening / starting the activity.
- Estimate turnover and profit realistically; do not artificially understate.
- Clarify income tax, trade tax, VAT, possible VAT ID for EU business and any cross-border services with a tax adviser.
- ELSTER form: https://www.elster.de/eportal/formulare-leistungen/alleformulare/fseeun
- Guidance: https://www.existenzgruendungsportal.de/gruenden-kurz-und-knapp/steuern

### Step 4 — Kleinunternehmer or regular VAT
For the German small-business VAT scheme under §19 UStG, the current statutory thresholds are:
- Prior calendar year's total turnover: no more than €25,000
- Current calendar year's total turnover: no more than €100,000

The scheme means qualifying domestic supplies are VAT-exempt; input VAT cannot be deducted. For B2B production clients, regular VAT can sometimes be commercially preferable because clients may deduct input VAT, while the small-business scheme can simplify administration. Make the choice after a turnover/cost forecast and tax advice.

Sources:
- https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ustg_1980/BJNR118130979.html
- https://www.finanzamt-bw.fv-bwl.de/,Lfr/Was+ist+die+sogenannte+Kleinunternehmerregelung_

### Step 5 — Bookkeeping
- Separate business bank account strongly recommended (not always legally mandatory for a sole proprietor).
- Record every income and expense from day one.
- Retain contracts, invoices, receipts and travel records.
- Use an EÜR if eligible; verify exact obligations with tax adviser.
- Reserve money for income tax and any VAT; do not treat all receipts as spendable profit.

## 6. Membership, insurance and compliance

### IHK / HWK
A commercial sole proprietor will generally become a member of the competent IHK or, depending on actual craft activities, HWK. Ask the local chamber about founder exemptions / reduced contributions and whether the proposed activity falls under IHK or HWK.

### Insurance to quote before first paid production
- Business / public liability (Betriebshaftpflicht), explicitly covering event production and technical work
- Professional indemnity / financial-loss cover if offering planning, programming or coordination
- Equipment cover only for gear actually owned or contractually controlled
- Legal expenses cover as a separate optional decision
- Accident cover / Berufsgenossenschaft classification check

Check policy exclusions for work at heights, rigging, electrical systems, pyrotechnics, temporary structures, international tours and shipboard work. Do not assume a standard office policy covers event operations.

### Website / legal notice
Before commercial public launch:
- Correct legal notice / Impressum with legal name and service address under §5 DDG
- Contact email and reliable contact route
- Privacy notice and lawful handling of contact-form data
- Cookie/analytics consent where required
- Terms, offer and cancellation terms suitable for B2B projects
- Rights / releases for every portfolio image, video, logo, music track and client reference

§5 DDG: https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/ddg/__5.html

## 7. Critical boundaries: referrals, crew and employee leasing

Start with a **partner-introduction model**, not an employee-leasing business.

Safer first model:
- Client contracts directly with the independent supplier.
- DARKTEC introduces the parties and, if agreed in advance, invoices a transparent referral/coordination fee for its own service.
- Written agreement defines what counts as a successful referral, the fee, payment trigger, cancellations, repeat business and disclosure to the client.
- Supplier independently controls its own business, pricing, delivery and tax obligations.

Do not recruit or employ crew and then assign them to clients under the client's direction without specialist advice. Employee leasing (Arbeitnehmerüberlassung) generally requires prior permission from the Federal Employment Agency. This is distinct from introducing independent businesses.

Official information: https://www.arbeitsagentur.de/unternehmen/personalfragen/pflichten-arbeitgeber/arbeitnehmerueberlassung/erlaubnis-arbeitnehmerueberlassung

### Scheinselbstständigkeit
For the founder's own technical gigs, the real working arrangement matters, not just the wording of the contract. Fixed shifts, extensive instructions, integration into the client's organisation and lack of entrepreneurial freedom can indicate dependent employment. Use the DRV self-check and, in uncertain cases, request a status determination.

- https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/DE/Rente/Arbeitnehmer-und-Selbststaendige/03_Selbststaendige/selbstcheck-erwerbsstatus.html
- https://www.deutsche-rentenversicherung.de/DRV/DE/Rente/Arbeitnehmer-und-Selbststaendige/03_Selbststaendige/statusfeststellungsverfahren.html

## 8. Employment aboard ships — pre-launch checks

Before taking paid DARKTEC work while employed aboard a cruise ship:
- Review the employment contract / crew handbook for secondary-employment, conflict-of-interest and approval rules.
- Do not use employer equipment, content, client lists, confidential material, shipboard time or brand assets for DARKTEC.
- Confirm availability and delivery commitments realistically around ship rotations.
- Ask a tax adviser to assess tax residence, place of management and any cross-border implications if the founder spends long periods outside Germany.
- Confirm health / pension / accident insurance implications of parallel employment and self-employment.

Do not assume that choosing a German business address alone settles tax residence or social-insurance status.

## 9. Lean financial plan — planning assumptions, not market quotes

Keep fixed costs low until demand is validated. Do not rent an office, buy a media server or hire staff at launch.

Indicative first-year setup budget to validate with actual local quotes:
- Registration, copies and administration: €50–€200
- Tax consultation / setup session: €250–€700
- Accounting / invoicing software: €0–€300 per year
- Business insurance: obtain quotations; reserve €300–€1,000+ annually depending on coverage and activities
- Website / domain / email: €50–€250 per year
- Portfolio / sales materials: €0–€300 if made in-house
- Working capital / travel / project expenses: set separately per job

**Planning reserve:** approximately €650–€2,750 plus working capital. These are budgeting placeholders, not quotes or guaranteed costs. Local fees and insurance depend on the actual scope.

### Revenue logic
Do not set final public prices until the service scope, market comparison and costs are clear. Build every quote from:
1. Required labour time and preparation
2. Travel, accommodation and per diem
3. Equipment rental / subcontractors
4. Insurance and admin overhead
5. Contingency and profit margin
6. VAT treatment

Track revenue, direct costs, gross margin, outstanding invoices and tax reserve monthly.

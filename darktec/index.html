01 / OPPORTUNITY

What DARKTEC is

An independent entertainment discovery project connecting live experiences, artists, organisers and production knowledge. It is growing in stages and is not presented as a registered agency or ticketing provider.

02 / OPPORTUNITY

What you can contribute

Suggest an event, propose an interview, share a verified project reference, submit an artist portfolio or discuss a partnership. Editorial consideration is not a guarantee of publication.

03 / OPPORTUNITY

The roadmap

We are building better event discovery, structured profiles, a useful contact process and a future AI assistant. Features will be introduced only when they can be operated responsibly.
